Lincoln Professional Academy

Professional programmes with training-provider partners

DKTCM works with training providers to design and run professional programmes that lead to a Lincoln University professional award, through Lincoln Professional Academy (LPA). Providers from any industry, including academies, corporate trainers, consultancies, technical centres, associations and vocational institutes, propose and deliver the courses. LPA reviews, approves, supervises and quality-assures the programme and administers certification.

DKTCM supports provider onboarding, corporate client introductions, learner recruitment, bilingual marketing and coordination between the parties.

The partnership process

How a provider programme comes to life

  1. Identify the provider — An academy, trainer or institution with a programme worth certifying.
  2. Proposal — The provider submits the course outline, delivery plan and assessment approach.
  3. Institutional review — LPA reviews the proposal against its requirements.
  4. Approval — Approval of the programme and its supervising arrangements.
  5. Preparation — Materials, schedule, assessment and learner documentation are finalised.
  6. Recruitment & delivery — Learners enrol and the provider delivers under LPA supervision.
  7. Assessment — Assessment is completed to the approved standard.
  8. Certification — Successful learners receive their professional award.
