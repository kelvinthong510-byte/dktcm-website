Insaniah Talent Hub Sdn Bhd

M&A, remittance, talent and engineering connections.

Insaniah Talent Hub Sdn Bhd provides M&A advisory and matchmaking, licensed money remittance, talent discovery, and engineering and industrial matchmaking. Introductions are a starting point: commercial evaluation, due diligence and transaction terms remain with the parties.

Services

Four lines of work

M&A advisory & matchmaking

Advisory and matchmaking between parties interested in the merger, acquisition or sale of businesses and commercial assets, through enquiry, introduction, negotiation and completion with the right professional counterparts.

Licensed money remittance

A licensed remittance service for clients who need a regulated transfer channel. Terms are confirmed directly with Insaniah.

Talent discovery

Consultancy connecting business needs with relevant talent, including cross-border and China-facing searches where role, language and working arrangement are defined first.

Engineering & industrial matchmaking

Connecting engineering and industrial sourcing needs, including industrial wear parts, with potential counterparties, judged on specification, lead time and commercial terms.

How a mandate runs

From brief to completion

  1. The brief — What is being bought, sold, sourced or hired, and on what terms a conversation is useful.
  2. Fit — Whether there is a genuine counterpart and both sides are ready to proceed.
  3. Introduction — A controlled introduction, with clear limits on what is shared at each stage.
  4. Diligence & terms — Coordination with legal and other advisers as information is exchanged and terms take shape.
  5. Completion — Support through to close, without confusing our role with that of counsel or the parties.
