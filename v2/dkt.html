DKT Consultancy & Management

The hub for education, consultancy and the group ecosystem.

DKT Consultancy & Management develops the education ecosystem, provides business consultancy, and coordinates the associated companies so each need reaches the company best placed to deliver it.

What DKT does

Four lines of work

Education ecosystem

Institutional partnerships, programme pathways and international education collaborations, including professional programmes with Lincoln Professional Academy.

Business consultancy

Practical consultancy for organisations entering a market, shaping a partnership or restructuring a process, with referral to the relevant associated company when the next step belongs there.

China partnership coordination

China education collaborations, institutional introductions and partnership conversations, working alongside Insaniah Talent Hub where transactions, remittance or industrial supply are involved.

Group ecosystem coordination

One point of contact that connects each need with the right company in the network and keeps multi-company work coherent.

Who we work with

Who this is for

  • Organisations that want a clear first conversation before committing to a specialist.
  • Training providers, academies and institutions seeking professional programme pathways and partnerships.
  • Businesses exploring China-related partnerships, introductions or project coordination.
  • Groups that need more than one capability from the network, coordinated in one place.
