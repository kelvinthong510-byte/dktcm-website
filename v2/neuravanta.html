Neuravanta Sdn Bhd

Corporate training and CIDB G7 project management.

Neuravanta Sdn Bhd is a corporate training and project management company holding CIDB G7 grade. It delivers corporate training and project management, and facilitates ADI, HRD Corp and PEKA programme and grant enquiries.

Enquire with NeuravantaView services
Services

What Neuravanta delivers

Corporate training

HRD Corp and ADI training aligned to a team's operational and development priorities, designed so it can actually be run on the floor.

Akademi Dalam Industri (ADI)

Consultation and implementation support for employers setting up an industry academy, from programme design to running it within the applicable rules.

PEKA & grant advisory

Facilitation of ADI, HRD Corp and PEKA programme and grant enquiries, with eligibility reviewed against each scheme's current requirements.

Project management (CIDB G7)

Project management services, engineering and outsourced project work from a company holding CIDB G7 grade, with scope and terms agreed in writing.

Funding note. Grant availability, HRD Corp claimability and approvals depend on the scheme, the employer and the programme. Facilitation is not a guarantee of funding or approval.

How we work

From need to programme

  1. Define the need — Industry, people, the capability gap, and any timing or funding constraint.
  2. Match the approach — Training, ADI, PEKA or a project scope, and the rules that will apply.
  3. Confirm the plan — Provider, responsibilities, costs and the documents required.
  4. Deliver — Run the programme or project as agreed.
  5. Close — Attendance, evidence and, where relevant, the paperwork needed for a claim.
