Carbon Ecology Research & Development Sdn Bhd

Sustaining people. Restoring futures.

Carbon Ecology Research & Development Sdn Bhd is a manpower supply and workforce development partner working alongside NT-2 Corporation. It helps organisations plan and build the workforce their operations need, including through rehabilitation and parole workforce programmes.

Enquire with Carbon EcologyView services
Services

What Carbon Ecology provides

Manpower supply

Foreign and local manpower for organisations planning their staffing requirements, with feasibility, documentation and service terms confirmed directly.

Rehabilitation & parole workforce programmes

Employment pathways for people in rehabilitation and parole programmes, matched to employers with genuine vacancies and prepared for the discipline of a worksite.

Workforce management

Management of client workforces under appointment, from documentation to day-to-day coordination with the employer.

Training & onboarding

Training, onboarding and support needs, linked with Neuravanta where relevant.

How a placement runs

From vacancy to a working arrangement

  1. The vacancy — The site, the role, the hours and the standards that will apply.
  2. Suitability — Identify candidates who can fairly be put forward.
  3. Preparation — Ready the people presented, so the first day is a job, not a surprise.
  4. Placement — Agree the arrangement and get the person into work.
  5. Stability — Stay in the conversation long enough for the job to last.
