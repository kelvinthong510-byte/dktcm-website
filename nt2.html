NT-2 Corporation Sdn Bhd

Manpower supply, handled properly.

NT-2 Corporation Sdn Bhd is a manpower agency providing foreign and local worker supply. It also supports social reintegration through the Ministry of Home Affairs OBB (Orang Bebas Berlesen) programme, giving businesses legal, reliable and socially responsible workforce support.

Enquire with NT-2 CorporationView services
Services

What NT-2 provides

Foreign worker recruitment

Legal placement of foreign workers in sectors such as manufacturing and construction, including support for eligible employers applying for quota.

Local worker supply

Local manpower for employers who need people on site, matched to role, location and start date.

OBB workforce support

Employment matching for former inmates under the Ministry of Home Affairs OBB programme.

Compliance & documentation

Handling of permits, contracts and regulatory requirements, so the arrangement can stand up to scrutiny.

Workforce planning

Staffing plans built around roles, location, skills and start dates.

How it works

A straightforward sequence

  1. Brief — Roles, site, numbers and timing.
  2. Eligibility — Whether the employer can properly apply, stated plainly.
  3. Application — Prepare and support the submission, keeping the employer informed.
  4. Placement — Coordinate arrival, contracts and the first period on site.
  5. Management — Ongoing documentation and support as agreed.
