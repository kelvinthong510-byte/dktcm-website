Who we are

A group built around how businesses actually grow.

A growing organisation rarely needs just one kind of help. It may need a training pathway for its people, workers for a new site, a counterpart for a transaction, or a sound structure for capital. Those are different crafts, and in our group they sit in different companies.

DKT Consultancy & Management is the hub. We provide the education ecosystem and business consultancy ourselves, and coordinate the associated companies so that each need is led by the company that holds the relevant licence and capability.

Why work with us

One conversation opens the whole network.

Bring the objective. We scope it, introduce the company best placed to lead, and keep the work coordinated through to delivery.

  1. Understand the need — One point of contact at DKTCM scopes your objective, context and timing.
  2. Match the right company — The associated company that holds the relevant licence and capability takes the lead.
  3. Coordinate delivery — DKTCM aligns the parties and follows the work through to completion.

Clear responsibilities

Every service is attributed to the company that delivers it. Scope, terms and the responsible party are confirmed before work begins.

Cross-border reach

Offices in Kuala Lumpur, Seremban, Pulau Indah and Labuan, with a Forest City presence in the Johor Special Economic Zone, serving Malaysia, Singapore and beyond.

Plain, careful claims

We describe programmes, funding schemes and financial services as they are, without promising approvals, grants or returns.
